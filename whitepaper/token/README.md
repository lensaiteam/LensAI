# The $LENS token

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Status: not launched.** There is no liquidity pool, no listing and no sale. Everything in this section describes a planned design for transparency. All figures may change before any launch. Nothing here is an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any token, a promise of value, or financial advice.
{% endhint %}

## Why the token waits

LensAI's first job is to prove that its calls hold up in public. A token launched before that proof would put a price chart in front of a scorecard that does not exist yet. So the order is deliberate:

1. **Product first.** Free calls, a public record, monthly scorecards.
2. **Proof next.** Months of unbroken, scored calls, misses included, and real usage.
3. **Token last.** Launched only when it has a job the product needs, and only after securities counsel review and an independent audit.

## Design principles

* **Calls and research stay free.** The token never gates a call, a brief, the record or the evidence behind them. Everyone gets the same call whether they hold $LENS or not.
* **A job a stablecoin cannot do.** Every use of $LENS is tied to something the network does: capacity, coordination, contribution or settlement between agents. If a stablecoin would do the job equally well, it is not a reason for the token.
* **No calls on $LENS.** The desk will never make a call on $LENS, by design and in code.
* **Fixed supply.** One billion tokens, minted once, with no emissions, no mint function, no transfer tax and no owner privileges.
* **Public vesting.** Every allocation vests on a published schedule, enforced by public on-chain vesting streams, so no insider unlocks ahead of the community and no schedule can be shortened after the fact.
* **No profit language.** $LENS is a utility and coordination token. It is not a share, not a claim on revenue and not an investment product.
