# Safeguards

A desk that makes public calls and is connected to a token has obvious conflicts of interest. LensAI handles them with rules that are enforced in code where possible and published where not.

## No calls on held tokens

The desk never makes a call on a token that the team, the treasury, partners, market makers or related projects hold or issue. **$LENS is first on that list.** This is enforced in the call policy: a call on a blocked asset is refused at the input, and a call whose text names one is turned into a no call.

## Disclosure

Before launch, LensAI will publish:

* who funds the project;
* team and related-project holdings, by token;
* related projects and the nature of the relationship;
* a personal-trading policy for the team, with blackout periods around calls.

Related projects are kept out of LensAI's own traction figures, and LensAI's data will not settle any related project's markets without a published independence policy.

## What the team will never do

* Trade ahead of, or against, the desk's own calls.
* Delete or quietly edit a call.
* Pay or reward anyone to post about LensAI or $LENS.
* Hint at exchange listings.
* Use $LENS to fund the free product.
* Make return claims, use profit language, or publish testimonials about the token.
* Ship a package or integration that asks a user for a private key.

## Before launch

* Securities counsel review of the token design and the call format.
* An independent audit of the token and vesting contracts.
* A trademark search and a settled ticker.
* Public vesting streams and the final schedule, published before any liquidity.
