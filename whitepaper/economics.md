# Economics

## Free for users

Calls and deep research are **free**. A paid "why is it moving" read is no longer a business: exchanges and data sites now give market explanations away. LensAI competes on what they do not offer, a committed call with a public record, and it keeps that free for three reasons:

1. **Reach.** A track record only matters if people see it. Free calls go where people already are: the desk, Telegram, X when tagged, partner apps and agents.
2. **Integrity.** Calls that cost nothing and go to everyone are impersonal market commentary, not a paid signal service.
3. **Data.** Every read and every question improves the evaluation set the desk is measured on.

### Guardrails on free

* Sign-in for deep reads; quick reads and the live pulse need none.
* Daily caps per account and basic bot checks.
* A monthly cost budget for model calls and licensed data.
* When capacity runs out, the desk serves the measured state and the shared briefs, which cost nothing, instead of failing.

## Cheap to serve by design

The cost of serving one more reader stays close to zero, because the expensive work is shared:

| Request | Cost per reader |
|---|---|
| Market State and generic token reads | None: one shared brief per hourly anchor, for everyone |
| What changed | None: arithmetic |
| Watches, however many | None: arithmetic on derived tables |
| The record | None: a read |
| A specific question | One structured generation, sometimes one small planning call |
| Claim Check | One structured generation |

Most reads make no model call at all. Margin widens with scale rather than narrowing.

### Known running costs

The main variable costs are model calls for specific questions and Claim Check, licensed cross-venue market data (liquidations, cross-venue open interest and order books, ETF flows), and the X API for Claim Check replies when tagged. Infrastructure is a single always-on engine service, a website, a user database and off-site backup.

## Who pays: businesses

When LensAI charges, it charges **businesses, not readers**. Each line meters something the engine already does:

| Line | What it is |
|---|---|
| **API and MCP volume** | Free access for agents up to a fair cap; metered access above it for platforms and products that build on the engine. |
| **Team desks** | Shared watches, alert routing to team channels, higher daily budgets and an audit trail, billed as a subscription. |
| **Data licensing** | Licensed, point-in-time access to the normalized cross-factor history, the corpus and the calibration record, the assets that cannot be rebuilt. |
| **Audit trails for regulated firms** | Firms increasingly have to answer for the AI tools they use. LensAI's hashed, append-only trail of what was said, on what evidence, is the kind of evidence they need. |
| **Partner widgets** | Market State and Claim Check widgets for exchanges, wallets and terminals: free in return for distribution and visible credit, paid at scale. |

## Funding the build

The free product is funded by grants, partnerships and investment, not by selling a token. $LENS is described in [the next section](token/README.md), and it is not used to fund the free product.
