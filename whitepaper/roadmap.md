# Roadmap

The order follows one constraint: **a track record cannot be backfilled.** So calls and public posting start first, coverage and distribution come next, and money and the token come last, once the scorecard can speak for itself.

Each phase ends at a gate. A gate is a checklist, not a date: if it is not met, the next phase waits.

## Live today

* The engine: immutable capture, the factor state store, the mechanism graph, the divergence engine, the tool layer and verified narration.
* The desk: product-first, with the live pulse, evidence blocks streamed as they are gathered, and answers with Answer, Evidence and Steps.
* Nine agents over one engine, including Watches and Claim Check.
* **The call:** every market and token read ends in one call or a counted no call, recorded in an append-only table.
* **Liquidation capture:** Bybit (complete) and Binance (sampled), live, with honest hourly coverage.

## Phase 1: the scored desk

* Post every call publicly as it is made, to the site's call log and a public channel.
* Resolve and score calls at their horizon; publish the first monthly scorecard, misses first.
* A "verify a call" page linking each call to its data, post and outcome.
* Email and Google sign-in; daily caps and bot checks for free deep research.
* Telegram and email delivery for Watches; a public status page.
* Macro inputs widened: oil, the 2-year yield, the CPI and FOMC calendar.

**Gate:** every call posted as made, two monthly scorecards published, calibration measured and published, user retention baseline measured.

## Phase 2: coverage and distribution

* The top 50 perpetual markets across Binance, Bybit, OKX and Hyperliquid.
* Licensed cross-venue liquidation, open-interest, order-book and ETF-flow data; auto-deleveraging data.
* Incident Mechanics as the flagship: a brief within 48 hours of every major break, and a permanent incident archive.
* Claim Check on Telegram and on X when tagged, from a labelled automated account; a public "most-checked claims" feed.
* A free, standard MCP server, listed in the official registry and offered to agent platforms.
* Design partners: Market State and Claim Check widgets for exchanges, wallets and terminals.
* Results published on open crypto-analysis benchmarks.

**Gate:** three or more incident briefs within 48 hours, Claim Check live on Telegram and X, at least one live integration, 99% published uptime.

## Phase 3: business and token

* Business lines: API and MCP volume, team desks, data licensing.
* Five or more live integrations and an unbroken public record since the first call.
* $LENS launch, only if the token has a job the product needs, after securities counsel review, an independent audit, public vesting streams and the disclosure policy.

**Gate:** calls at each confidence level come true at about that rate (calls at 70% about 70% of the time), five integrations live, 99.5% uptime.

## Metrics we will report

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Calls posted publicly as made | Whether the record is complete |
| Monthly scorecards published, misses included | Whether the record is honest |
| Hit rate by confidence level | Whether the desk is calibrated |
| Weekly users active on two or more days | Whether people come back |
| Claim checks per week | Whether the desk is useful where claims spread |
| Incident briefs within 48 hours | Whether the flagship delivers |
| Published 30-day uptime | Whether agents can rely on it |
