# Value flow

{% hint style="info" %}
Planned design. Shares and mechanisms are illustrative until launch and subject to counsel review.
{% endhint %}

Revenue comes from businesses: API and MCP volume, team desks, data licensing and partner integrations. It flows in a fixed order:

1. **The pipeline first.** Live data, licensed feeds, model calls and infrastructure: the real cost of keeping the desk running and free for readers.
2. **The treasury next.** A published share of business revenue goes to the treasury, which funds coverage expansion, data sources, audits and grants under governance.
3. **Buybacks only by rule.** If the treasury buys back $LENS, it does so only from realised business revenue, under a published rule, never from token sales and never at discretion. Every buyback is reported net of the unlocks in the same period, so the supply picture is honest.

## Supply honesty

Buybacks are only meaningful when they are set against unlocks. Each quarterly report will show, side by side:

* tokens unlocked in the period, by allocation;
* tokens bought back, with the revenue that funded them;
* the net change in circulating supply.

## What does not happen

* No revenue share, dividend or yield is paid to holders.
* No buyback is funded by selling tokens or by the free product's budget.
* No figure in this paper is a projection of price or return.
