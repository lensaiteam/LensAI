01 What is CryptoLens AI?

CryptoLens AI is a token intelligence platform that combines live on-chain data from DexScreener with Llama 3.3 70B AI (via Groq) to give you a complete research report on any crypto token in under 30 seconds.

It scans price, volume, liquidity, holder distribution, whale activity, tokenomics, social sentiment then generates AI-written scores, red flag alerts, and a final verdict. Think of it as a personal analyst available 24/7.

Free to use. 5 AI-powered analyses every day no signup, no credit card. Just open the Research Terminal and start.
Not financial advice. Scores and AI summaries are research aids not investment recommendations. Always apply your own judgment.

04 AI Score System

Every token gets 4 AI-generated scores on a 0–100 scale, calculated using live on-chain data, tokenomics, team signals, and social sentiment from Llama 3.3 70B.

Safety Score100
Honeypot risk, contract verification, ownership concentration, rug pull signals. Most important score.
Team Score100
Doxxed members, social presence, past project history, community trust signals.
Tokenomics Score100
Supply distribution, vesting schedules, inflation rate, LP lock status, holder concentration.
Community Score100
Social sentiment, holder growth trend, engagement across Twitter, Telegram, Discord.
Always check Safety Score first. A token with 90+ Community but 20 Safety is a classic pump-and-dump setup. Safety is the single most important number.
05 Red Flag Detection

CryptoLens AI automatically scans for common scam patterns. These are the flags that matter most watch for Critical ones especially.

Honeypot Detected
Contract blocks selling you can buy but never exit. Do not invest under any circumstances.
Critical
Unverified Contract
Source code not published on block explorer. Impossible to audit what it actually does to your funds.
Critical
Top 10 Wallets >80%
Extreme concentration any whale can dump and crash price instantly. No protection for retail holders.
Critical
LP Not Locked
Liquidity pool removable by team at any time. Classic rug pull mechanism extreme caution required.
Warning
No Audit + No Doxx
No third-party security audit and fully anonymous team. Very high risk combination for newer tokens.
Warning
Mint Authority Active
Team can print new tokens, diluting your position. Only acceptable with credible locked vesting schedule.
Warning
06 Reading the Verdict

Every analysis ends with an Overall Score (0–100) and an AI-written verdict. Here is what each score range means:

75+
Safe to Research
Strong fundamentals, clean contract, good team signals. Use as a starting point not a buy signal.
40–74
Caution
Mixed signals some positives but notable risks. Size positions conservatively if investing.
0–39
High Risk / Avoid
Multiple red flags detected. High probability of scam, rug pull, or total loss. Avoid.
High score ≠ buy signal. Even 90+ doesn't guarantee returns. Market conditions, timing, and your own risk tolerance all matter independently.
