§ 01 The Problem

Retail always
finds out

The crypto market is structurally unfair to retail participants. Not because of malice but because of infrastructure gaps that compound over time. Those with access to on-chain scanners, whale wallet trackers, and real-time contract auditing tools consistently act before prices reflect new information.

The result is predictable: retail buys after the pump, sells into whale distribution, and falls into honeypot traps that more sophisticated analysis would have flagged in seconds.
